# Demo Script

1. Open the dashboard and show tracked students, activity records, low lab supplies, and at-risk students.
2. Click `Indexed documents Show`, then hide it again to show the cleaner layout.
3. Click `School Records`.
4. Point out that the agent selected a school record tool and returned evidence.
5. Ask: `Aling lab supplies ang paubos na?`
6. Ask: `Show project scores greater than 85.`
7. Click `School Docs` and show retrieved policy/context snippets.
8. Click `Forecasts` and explain that the backend uses a scikit-learn forecasting model when available.
9. Click `Agent Console`, then ask: `Generate a school report.`
10. Show the summary, recommendations, chart, evidence, retrieved document snippets, confidence badge, and agent workflow steps.
11. Click `Download report` to show that the agent can produce a reusable school report.
12. Open `Add School Data`.
13. Add a new lab supply item.
14. Click the save notification to open the exact added item and notes.
15. Ask again: `Aling lab supplies ang paubos na?` and show that the new item is included in the answer.
16. Show that suggested questions disappear after a chat, then click `Need recommended questions? Yes` to bring them back.
